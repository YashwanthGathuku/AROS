7. **Project-claim campaigns.** The useful next product step after the certificate is to turn a checkout's own stated guarantees (routes, tests, comments) into campaigns, with Rust still deciding the oracle. Target text stays untrusted. No public-internet discovery.

8. **Keep the original tree unmodified, the harness out of the target repo, and the LLM out of the verdict.** Those are the invariants that similar tools do not combine.
